a(SOWS_NPD) Provides the total number of non-productive days of sows in the reporting interval. Sow is considered productive when it is pregnant or lactating. Non-productive days are between last weaning and next insemination, dead or selling for slaughter and between insemination and some kind of failure like failed insemination, abortion, dead or sold for slaughter. Weaning day is considered as a productive day based on farm setting `Is weaning a productive day`.
b(SOWS_REAL_FEEDING_DAYS) This KPI gives the total number of feeding days for all sows, including inseminated gilts, when counting only sow‑days in sow locations. It adds “real” feeding days for sows (parity ≥1) and for parity‑0 sows. This excludes time that young breeding animals spend in non‑sow locations. A higher value means animals spend more days as sows in sow facilities during the interval.
c(LITTER_SOW_YEAR) The average number of litter per sow per year.
d(SOWS_FEEDING_DAYS) This KPI gives the total number of feeding days for all sows, including inseminated gilts, during the interval. It adds sow feeding days (parity ≥1) and parity‑0 sow feeding days. A feeding day is counted for each day an animal is alive and fed in the relevant locations. The value increases with either more animals or a longer time span in which they are present.

BATCH_DEAD_LATE_AMOUNT: This KPI counts pigs that died in the batch after 70 days of age. Only deaths where age at death is more than 70 days are included. It reflects late‑phase mortality close to the end of the production cycle.

BATCH_LATE_MORTALITY_PCT: This KPI shows the percentage of batch deaths that happened after 70 days of age. It divides late deaths by total deaths and multiplies by 100; if there are no deaths, it returns zero. The KPI indicates how much mortality occurs late in the batch.
BATCH_LENGTH: This KPI gives the calendar duration of the batch in days. It is the number of days between the batch start date and the batch end date. It does not depend on how many pigs were present; it reflects only the time span of the batch.

BATCH_LIVE_SELL_TOTAL_WEIGHT: This indicator shows the total live weight of pigs from the batch that were sold as live animals in the interval. It adds live weights from individual-ID live sales and group live sales. Slaughter sales are excluded. The result is in the same weight unit as the sale records.
BATCH_MARGIN_CONTRIBUTION: This KPI shows the margin contribution for the batch in absolute monetary terms. It subtracts total batch costs from total batch revenue. A positive value means revenue exceeded costs, while a negative value indicates a loss. It does not include any overhead allocation beyond the costs entered for this batch.
BATCH_MARGIN_CONTRIBUTION_PER_ONE_PIG: This KPI shows the average margin contribution per pig sold from the batch. It divides the batch margin contribution by the number of pigs sold. If no pigs were sold, the KPI is set to zero. The result shows how much profit or loss is created per marketed pig after direct batch costs.
BATCH_MARGIN_CONTRIBUTION_PER_ONE_WEIGHT_GAIN_UNIT: This KPI shows the margin contribution per unit of live weight gain in the batch. It divides the margin contribution by the total live weight gain produced. If there is no recorded gain, the KPI is set to zero. The result indicates how efficiently weight gain is converted into profit after direct costs.
BATCH_MARGIN_CONTRIBUTION_PER_ONE_WEIGHT_UNIT: This KPI shows the margin contribution per unit of live weight sold from the batch. It divides the margin contribution by the total live weight of pigs sold. If no live weight was sold, the KPI is set to zero. This expresses how much margin is achieved per kilogram (or other weight unit) of sold weight.
BATCH_MARGIN_CONTRIBUTION_SOLD_RATIO: This KPI expresses the batch margin contribution as a percentage of the sales revenue. It divides the margin contribution by total sales revenue and multiplies by 100. If no sales revenue exists, the KPI is set to zero to avoid division by zero. A higher percentage means a larger part of revenue remains after direct batch costs.
BATCH_MEDICINE_PRICE: This KPI shows the total cost of medicines used for animals in the batch during the interval. It directly uses the sum of all medicine costs linked to the batch. No additional scaling or division is applied. The result is the gross medicine expense for the batch.
BATCH_MID_MORTALITY_PCT: This KPI shows the percentage of batch deaths that happened between 40 and 70 days of age. BATCH_MORTALITY_OF_STARTED_PIGS_PCT: This KPI shows the mortality percentage among pigs that started the batch. It divides the total number of dead pigs in the batch by the number of accepted delivered pigs and multiplies by 100. The result expresses what fraction of starting pigs died during the batch, in percent.
BATCH_NOT_ACCEPTABLE_AMOUNT: This KPI counts pigs from the purchase delivery that were marked as not acceptable for the batch. These pigs arrived but were rejected rather than entered into the batch. The value helps separate rejected pigs from accepted ones and from those that died under transport.
BATCH_OTHER_COST_PRICE: This KPI shows the total amount of other (non‑feed, non‑medicine) costs assigned to the batch. It directly returns the sum of these costs for the batch. There is no division by pig numbers or weights in this KPI. It represents the full other‑cost burden on the batch.
BATCH_PERIOD_DAYS: This KPI shows the length of the reporting period considered for the batch in days. It may differ from the full batch length if you are analysing only part of the batch lifetime. It is used as a time reference for other batch‑based calculations.
BATCH_PIG_DAYS: This KPI represents the total pig‑days in the batch (pigs × days). It sums, for all pigs, the number of days each pig spent in the batch, regardless of feeding. BATCH_SELL_AVG_AGE: This indicator shows the average age in days of pigs sold from the batch in the interval. It divides the total age of all sold pigs in the batch by the number of sold pigs with recorded age. If no pigs with age were sold, the value is set to zero. The unit is days.

BATCH_SELL_TOTAL_PRICE: This KPI gives the total sale revenue from pigs sold in the batch. It adds together income from slaughter sales and live sales. The value does not include internal transfer values or purchase costs, only outgoing sale revenue.
BATCH_SELL_TOTAL_WEIGHT: This indicator shows the total live weight of all pigs sold from the batch in the interval. BATCH_SLAUGHTER_SELL_AVG_WEIGHT: This indicator shows the average live weight per pig for slaughter pigs sold from the batch in the interval.
