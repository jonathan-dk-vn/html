Unit 9 · Destination B2

-ING vs TO-INFINITIVE
Cuộc "Đối Đầu" Kinh Điển

"I enjoy to read" hay "I enjoy reading"? "I want going" hay "I want to go"? Đây chính là cuộc đối đầu kinh điển khiến hàng triệu học viên "đau đầu" nhất. Nhưng đừng lo — chúng ta sẽ giải mã tất cả!

9.1

Verb + -ING Form — Động Từ + V-ing

🤔 Mở đầu: Tại sao "I finished to write" nghe sai sai?
Bản chất: Một số động từ "thích" có -ING theo sau, như những cặp bạn thân không thể tách rời!

📝 Danh sách động từ + V-ING:

Nhóm 1 · Cảm xúc & Thái độ
likeloveenjoyhatedislikedetestmindcan't help
I enjoy reading novels.  ·  Do you mind opening the window?
Nhóm 2 · Bắt đầu & Kết thúc
startbeginfinishstopkeep (on)give up
I finished writing my essay.  ·  She keeps talking in class.
Nhóm 3 · Hoãn & Tránh
postponedelayput offavoidescape
We postponed meeting until tomorrow.  ·  I avoid walking alone at night.
Nhóm 4 · Khác
admitdenysuggestriskinvolve
He denied stealing the money.  ·  This job involves traveling a lot.
🔥 Đặc Điểm Quan Trọng

Sau giới từ → LUÔN V-ING:

  • I'm interested in learning French.
  • Thank you for helping me.
  • I'm tired of waiting.

Có thể có tân ngữ trước V-ING:

  • I can't stand people cheating in exams.
  • I don't mind him coming late.
⚠️ LỖI SAI THƯỜNG GẶP
Lỗi 1 — Dùng to-infinitive thay V-ING:
❌I enjoy to read books.
✅I enjoy reading books.
Lỗi 2 — Quên V-ING sau giới từ:
❌I'm good at to cook.
✅I'm good at cooking.
9.2

Verb + TO-Infinitive — Động Từ + To + V

🤔 Mở đầu: Tại sao "I want going" nghe kỳ kỳ?
Bản chất: Một số động từ "thích" có TO-infinitive theo sau!

📝 Danh sách động từ + TO-Infinitive:

Nhóm 1 · Ý định & Quyết định
wantwishwould likedecidechooseplanhopeexpect
I want to travel abroad.  ·  She decided to quit her job.
Nhóm 2 · Cố gắng & Thành công
tryattemptmanagefailtend
I managed to finish on time.  ·  He failed to pass the exam.
Nhóm 3 · Lời hứa & Đồng ý
promiseagreeofferrefusepretend
I promise to help you.  ·  She refused to answer.
Nhóm 4 · Tính từ + To
ablepleasedreadyhappyexcitedsurprised
I'm able to swim.  ·  I'm happy to meet you.
🔥 Có Thể Có Tân Ngữ
  • I want to call my mom.
  • I want you to call me. (tân ngữ xen vào)
  • She encouraged me to apply for the job.
⚠️ LỖI SAI THƯỜNG GẶP
Lỗi 1 — Dùng V-ING thay TO-infinitive:
❌I want going home.
✅I want to go home.
Lỗi 2 — Quên "to":
❌I decided go there.
✅I decided to go there.
9.3

Verb + Bare Infinitive — Không Có "To"

🤔 Mở đầu: Khi nào "to" biến mất?
Bản chất: Một số động từ đặc biệt "ăn mất" chữ "to"!

📝 Danh sách động từ + Bare Infinitive:

Modal Verbs (đã học Unit 7)
cancouldwillwouldshouldmustmaymight
I can swim.  ·  You should study.
Động từ tri giác + Object
seehearwatchnoticefeel
I saw him leave.  ·  I heard her cry.
Động từ khiến + Object
makelet
She made me cry.  ·  Let me help you.

🔥 Phân biệt Bare Infinitive vs V-ING với động từ tri giác:

Bare Infinitive (hoàn chỉnh) V-ING (đang diễn ra)
I heard him tell the story.
Nghe toàn bộ câu chuyện 		I heard him telling the story.
Nghe một phần câu chuyện
I saw her cross the road.
Thấy cả quá trình		 I saw her crossing the road.
Thấy đang băng qua
🔥 Passive của make/hear/see → TO-Infinitive!

Active: The teacher made me stand up.

Passive: I was made to stand up.

⚠️ LỖI SAI THƯỜNG GẶP
Lỗi 1 — Thêm "to" sau make/let:
❌She made me to cry.
✅She made me cry.
Lỗi 2 — Quên "to" trong passive:
❌I was made stand up.
✅I was made to stand up.
9.4

Verb + TO-Inf hoặc V-ING — Ít Khác Biệt

🤔 Mở đầu: Khi nào cả hai đều đúng?
Bản chất: Một số động từ "thoải mái" với cả hai — ý nghĩa gần như không đổi.

📝 Danh sách "động từ dễ tính":

begin start continue can't bear can't stand hate intend love prefer
  • Start — cả hai đều đúng: I started to learn / started learning English.
  • Love — ý nghĩa giống nhau: She loves to dance / loves dancing.
💡 Mẹo Ghi Nhớ

Những động từ này "dễ tính" — dùng cái nào cũng được!

9.5

Verb + TO-Inf hoặc V-ING — Khác Nghĩa Hoàn Toàn!

🤔 Mở đầu: "I remember to do" vs "I remember doing" — Cái nào đúng?
Bản chất: Đây là những động từ "khó tính" — thay đổi form là thay đổi nghĩa!

📊 Bảng phân biệt chi tiết:

Động từ + TO-INFINITIVE + V-ING
remember Nhớ phải làm (tương lai)
Remember to lock the door!		 Nhớ đã làm (quá khứ)
I remember locking the door.
forget Quên phải làm
Don't forget to call me!		 Quên đã làm
I'll never forget meeting you.
stop Dừng để làm việc khác
I stopped to buy coffee.		 Dừng việc đang làm
I stopped smoking.
try Cố gắng làm
Try to be on time!		 Thử làm (xem sao)
Try calling him.
regret Tiếc phải làm
I regret to tell you…		 Tiếc đã làm
I regret buying this car.

🎯 Phân tích chi tiết từng động từ:

REMEMBER:

Remember + TO (tương lai)
Nhớ phải làm gì đó
Did you remember to do your homework?
(Bạn có nhớ làm bài không?)
Remember + ING (quá khứ)
Nhớ đã làm gì rồi
I remember doing my homework.
(Tôi nhớ đã làm bài rồi.)

STOP:

Stop + TO (mục đích)
Dừng để làm việc khác
I stopped to talk to him.
(Tôi dừng lại để nói chuyện)
Stop + ING (chấm dứt)
Ngừng việc đang làm
I stopped talking to him.
(Tôi ngừng nói chuyện)

TRY:

Try + TO (cố gắng)
Nỗ lực để đạt kết quả
Try to arrive on time.
(Cố gắng đến đúng giờ)
Try + ING (thử nghiệm)
Thử một cách tiếp cận
Try taking this medicine.
(Thử uống thuốc này xem)
⚠️ LỖI SAI THƯỜNG GẶP
Lỗi 1 — Nhầm lẫn remember:
❌I remember to meet him yesterday.
✅I remember meeting him yesterday.
(muốn nói: nhớ đã gặp anh ấy)
Lỗi 2 — Nhầm lẫn stop:
❌I stopped to smoke.
✅I stopped smoking.
(muốn nói: bỏ thuốc lá)
9.6

Prefer, Would Rather, Had Better — Thích Hơn & Lời Khuyên

🤔 Mở đầu: Ba cách diễn đạt "thích hơn" — khi nào dùng cái nào?
PREFER
prefer + noun/V-ing + to + noun/V-ing
Thích hơn (chung chung, thói quen)
I prefer coffee to tea.
I prefer reading to watching TV.
WOULD PREFER
would prefer + to-inf + rather than + inf
Thích hơn (lần này cụ thể)
I'd prefer to stay rather than go.
I'd prefer to have coffee.
WOULD RATHER
would rather + bare inf + than + bare inf
Thích hơn (chung hoặc cụ thể)
I'd rather stay than take the bus.
I'd rather you came tomorrow.
HAD BETTER
had better + bare infinitive
Lời khuyên mạnh (có hậu quả)
You'd better ask your parents first.
We'd better not be late.

🎯 Ví dụ chi tiết từng cấu trúc:

PREFER (thói quen chung):

  • Danh từ / V-ING + to: I prefer reading to watching TV.
  • Phủ định: She prefers not to work late.

WOULD RATHER (lần này / muốn ai đó làm):

  • Cá nhân — bare infinitive: I'd rather walk than take the bus.
  • Muốn ai đó làm — past simple: I'd rather you came tomorrow. Chú ý: Dùng Past Simple để diễn đạt yêu cầu lịch sự!

HAD BETTER (lời khuyên mạnh):

  • Lời khuyên có hậu quả: You'd better hurry up! Phân biệt: Should (nhẹ) → Had better (mạnh hơn, ngụ ý hậu quả)
⚠️ LỖI SAI THƯỜNG GẶP
Lỗi 1 — Dùng "I don't prefer":
❌I don't prefer coffee.
✅I prefer not to drink coffee.
Lỗi 2 — Nhầm lẫn had better với should:
📌You should study.
(lời khuyên nhẹ)
⚡You'd better study.
(lời khuyên mạnh — ngụ ý hậu quả)
9.7

Infinitives of Purpose — Diễn Đạt Mục Đích

🤔 Mở đầu: "Tại sao" làm việc gì đó?
Bản chất: Diễn tả mục đích, lý do thực hiện hành động.

📝 Ba cách diễn đạt:

to + infinitive I went to university to avoid getting a job!
in order + to I study hard in order to pass the exam.
so as + to I got up early so as to catch the bus.

🔄 So sánh với "so that":

  • So that (+ clause): I study hard so that I can pass the exam.
  • To (+ infinitive): I study hard to pass the exam.

🔥 Phủ định mục đích:

  • In order not to (✅ đúng): I left early in order not to be late.
  • So as not to (✅ đúng): I left early so as not to be late.
⚠️ LỖI SAI THƯỜNG GẶP
Lỗi — Dùng "not to" đơn độc cho mục đích phủ định:
❌I left early not to be late.
✅I left early in order not to be late.
✅I left early so as not to be late.
💡 Mẹo Ghi Nhớ
  • TO + infinitive: Đơn giản nhất — dùng hàng ngày
  • IN ORDER TO / SO AS TO: Trang trọng hơn — văn viết
  • Phủ định: Phải có "in order" hoặc "so as" trước "not to"

🎯 Sơ Đồ Quyết Định -ING vs TO-Infinitive
-ING vs TO-INFINITIVE — Chọn gì? 🤔

Sau giới từ?                → V-ING  (always!)
Enjoy, finish, avoid...?   → V-ING
Want, decide, hope...?     → TO-INFINITIVE
Make, let, modal verbs?    → BARE INFINITIVE
Remember/forget/stop/try?  → XEM NGHĨA!  (cả hai nhưng nghĩa khác)
Start/begin/continue/love? → CẢ HAI OK   (ít khác biệt)

📊 Bảng tham khảo nhanh

Loại Động từ Ví dụ
+ V-ING enjoy, finish, avoid, mind enjoy reading
+ TO-INF want, decide, hope, manage want to go
+ BARE INF can, make, let, see, hear can swim, make me cry
Cả hai (khác nghĩa) remember, forget, stop, try remember to do vs doing
Cả hai (giống nghĩa) start, begin, continue, love start to work = start working

📖 My Hobbies and Goals

I've always enjoyed exploring new hobbies, and I want to share how these activities have shaped my life goals. When I was younger, I couldn't stand sitting still – I needed to be constantly active. My parents made me try various activities, and I'm grateful they let me explore different interests. I remember trying piano lessons at age seven, but I stopped playing after just six months. Now I regret giving up so easily, and I often wish to go back in time to tell my younger self to continue practicing. These days, I prefer learning at my own pace rather than follow rigid schedules. I've decided to pursue hobbies that genuinely interest me, and I hope to develop skills that will last a lifetime.

Photography has become my greatest passion. I started taking photos five years ago to capture memories from a trip to Iceland. Initially, I avoided sharing my work because I couldn't help comparing myself to professionals. But I kept practicing, and eventually I managed to overcome my insecurity. I love wandering through the city looking for interesting subjects. Sometimes I stop to photograph unexpected moments – a child feeding pigeons, an elderly couple holding hands, or sunlight streaming through buildings. I've learned to appreciate the beauty in everyday scenes. My friends encouraged me to enter competitions, and though I failed to win at first, I refused to give up. I remember winning my first award last year – I'll never forget seeing my photograph displayed in a gallery.

Music remains important in my life, though my approach has changed. I began learning guitar two years ago, and this time I promised to stick with it. I try to practice for at least thirty minutes daily. Sometimes I feel like quitting when chords seem impossible, but I force myself to continue. I can't help smiling when I finally master a difficult song. My teacher suggested joining a band, but I prefer playing alone for now. I'd rather improve my skills before performing publicly. However, I intend to start busking next summer to gain confidence. I need to overcome my stage fright, and there's no better way than facing it directly. My goal is to be able to play at open mic nights without feeling nervous.

Writing has always been something I wanted to pursue seriously. I enjoy creating stories and exploring different perspectives through characters. Every morning, I try writing for an hour before work. Some days the words flow easily; other days I struggle to produce a single paragraph. I've started attending workshops to improve my technique. The instructor keeps telling us to write without worrying about perfection. She made us promise not to edit while drafting. I remember hesitating at first, but I've learned to let my creativity flow freely. I hope to finish my novel this year, and I plan to submit it to publishers. I'd rather face rejection than wonder what might have been.

Physical fitness has become essential to maintaining my mental health. I used to hate exercising, but I decided to change my attitude. I started by walking every morning, then gradually began running. Now I can't imagine starting my day without a workout. I love feeling the endorphins after a good run. My doctor suggested trying yoga to improve flexibility, and I agreed to give it a chance. At first, I couldn't help laughing at myself attempting difficult poses. But I kept going, and now I manage to complete entire sessions without stopping. I prefer working out alone, but I might consider joining a gym to access better equipment. My goal is to be able to run a marathon by next year.

Cooking has evolved from necessity to passion. I started learning out of necessity when I moved out, but I've grown to love experimenting with flavors. I enjoy trying recipes from different cultures. Sometimes experiments fail to turn out as expected, but I don't mind making mistakes – they're part of learning. I remember attempting to make sushi for the first time and ending up with a mess. But I refused to be discouraged. I kept practicing, and now friends ask me to cook for gatherings. I prefer cooking for others to eating alone. There's something satisfying about watching people enjoy food I've prepared. I want to learn professional techniques, so I'm considering taking culinary classes.

Travel photography combines two of my passions. I love exploring new places while capturing their essence through my lens. I try to avoid typical tourist shots, preferring to find unique perspectives. Before trips, I spend hours researching locations and planning shots. However, I've learned to remain flexible – sometimes the best photos come from wandering without a plan. I stopped trying to photograph everything and started focusing on quality over quantity. I'd rather take ten meaningful photos than hundreds of forgettable ones. My dream is to become a travel photographer, though I realize achieving this goal will require dedication. I need to build a strong portfolio and learn to market myself effectively.

Language learning has taught me patience and persistence. I began studying Spanish three years ago to communicate with my partner's family. Initially, I kept making the same mistakes and felt like giving up. But I remembered reading that language acquisition takes time. I tried using various apps, but I prefer taking structured classes. My teacher encourages us to speak without worrying about errors. She lets us make mistakes and gently corrects us. I can't stand memorizing grammar rules in isolation, so I prefer learning through context. I make myself practice daily, even when I don't feel motivated. My goal is to achieve fluency within two years. I'd better start reading Spanish literature to improve my vocabulary.

Volunteering has given my hobbies deeper meaning. I started teaching photography to underprivileged youth last year. Initially, I worried about being qualified enough, but the organization assured me that enthusiasm matters more than expertise. I love seeing the kids discover their creative potential. Some of them had never held a camera before. I let them experiment freely while offering guidance when needed. One student told me she wants to become a photographer after participating in our program. Moments like these make me realize the impact of sharing our passions. I plan to continue volunteering and hope to expand the program to more communities.

Personal growth through hobbies has taught me valuable lessons. I've learned to embrace failure as part of the learning process. I used to avoid trying new things for fear of looking foolish. Now I understand failing is necessary for growth. Each hobby has helped me develop different skills – photography taught patience, music taught discipline, writing taught persistence. I prefer learning through experience to reading about theories. I'd rather make mistakes and learn from them than never try at all. I keep reminding myself that mastery takes time. Rome wasn't built in a day, and I shouldn't expect to become an expert overnight.

Looking ahead, I want to continue exploring new interests while deepening existing ones. I'm considering learning pottery next year to explore three-dimensional creativity. I might try acting classes to overcome my remaining shyness. Whatever I choose, I promise to approach it with an open mind. I've stopped comparing myself to others and started focusing on personal progress. I'd better stop procrastinating and start pursuing these goals actively. Life is too short to postpone doing what we love. I encourage everyone to find hobbies that bring joy and to keep exploring new possibilities. After all, we never stop learning and growing through our passions.

Grammar Features Highlighted

Verb + -ing: Correctly used after enjoy, avoid, can't help, finish, keep, suggest, mind, can't stand, prefer, love (enjoyed exploring, avoid sharing, keep practicing, suggest joining).

Verb + to-infinitive: Properly used after want, decide, hope, manage, promise, plan, refuse, fail, need (want to share, decided to pursue, hope to develop, managed to overcome).

Bare infinitive: Used after make, let, modal verbs, and perception verbs (made me try, let me explore, can't imagine, watch people enjoy).

After prepositions: Always -ing form (to maintaining, about being, without worrying, by walking, for looking).

Different meanings: Remember/forget/stop/try with both forms showing meaning changes (remember trying vs remember to practice; stop to photograph vs stopped trying; try to practice vs try writing).

Same meaning: Start/begin/continue/love with both forms (started taking / began learning, love wandering).

Prefer/Would rather/Had better: All structures demonstrated (prefer learning to reading, rather improve…before performing, better start reading).

Infinitives of purpose: To/in order to/so as to (to capture memories, to gain confidence, to improve flexibility).

Common Errors Avoided: No to-infinitive after enjoy, finish, avoid; no -ing after want, decide, hope; no "to" after make/let; always -ing after prepositions; correct distinction between remember/stop/try meanings.
