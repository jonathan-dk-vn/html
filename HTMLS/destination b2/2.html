CÁC THÌ QUÁ KHỨ
Past Tenses

Bạn có nhận ra rằng mỗi khi kể về kỳ nghỉ, chia sẻ kinh nghiệm, hay thậm chí viết CV, chúng ta đều phải sử dụng các thì quá khứ? Đây chính là "công cụ kể chuyện" mạnh mẽ nhất trong tiếng Anh!

2.1

Past Simple — Thì Quá Khứ Đơn

🤔 Mở đầu: "I went to Paris last year" — Tại sao lại đơn giản vậy?
Bản chất của Past Simple: Đây là thì "chụp ảnh" — nó ghi lại những hành động đã hoàn tất trong quá khứ, như những bức ảnh trong album kỷ niệm.

📝 Công thức:

Loại câu Tất cả chủ ngữ
Khẳng định S + V-ed / V2
Phủ định S + didn't + V (nguyên mẫu)
Nghi vấn Did + S + V (nguyên mẫu)?

🎯 Các cách dùng:

  • 1. Hành động đơn lẻ đã hoàn thành: Tom and I played a game of chess and he won. Phân tích: Hai hành động liên tiếp: chơi cờ → thắng thua
  • 2. Thói quen trong quá khứ: Did you collect stamps when you were younger? So sánh: Bây giờ không sưu tập nữa!
  • 3. Tình huống cố định trong quá khứ: A famous footballer lived in our house before we bought it.
  • 4. Sự thật trong quá khứ: Crosswords didn't become popular until the 1930s.
  • 5. Chuỗi sự kiện chính trong câu chuyện: The referee blew the whistle and Simon passed the ball to James, who ran towards the goal. Đặc điểm: Như một bộ phim — sự kiện nối tiếp sự kiện!
🔥 Tính Năng Đặc Biệt — Nhấn Mạnh Với "did"

"Why didn't you win yesterday?" "I DID win. Who told you I didn't?"

⚠️ LỖI SAI THƯỜNG GẶP
Lỗi 1 — Quên biến đổi động từ bất quy tắc:
❌I goed to the store.
✅I went to the store.
Lỗi 2 — Dùng sai trợ động từ:
❌Did he went there?
✅Did he go there?
Lỗi 3 — Nhầm lẫn với Present Perfect:
❌I have seen him yesterday.
✅I saw him yesterday.
💡 Mẹo Ghi Nhớ
  • Dấu hiệu: yesterday last week/month/year ago in 1990 when I was young
  • Quy tắc: Có thời gian cụ thể trong quá khứ → Past Simple!
2.2

Past Continuous — Thì Quá Khứ Tiếp Diễn

🤔 Mở đầu: "I was sleeping when you called" — Tại sao không phải "I slept"?
Bản chất: Giống như "camera quay phim" trong quá khứ — ghi lại hành động đang diễn ra tại một thời điểm cụ thể trong quá khứ.

📝 Công thức:

Loại câu I / He / She / It You / We / They
Khẳng định was + V-ing were + V-ing
Phủ định wasn't + V-ing weren't + V-ing
Nghi vấn Was + S + V-ing? Were + S + V-ing?

🎯 Các cách dùng:

  • 1. Hành động tại thời điểm cụ thể: At five o'clock, I was reading my new book. Hình dung: Lúc 5h, bạn "đông cứng" trong tư thế đọc sách!
  • 2. Tình huống tạm thời trong quá khứ: Greg was living in London at the time.
  • 3. Thói quen khó chịu (với "always"): My brother was always borrowing my toys. Ý nghĩa: Thể hiện sự bực bội trong quá khứ!
  • 4. Hành động kéo dài: Daniel was playing video games all morning yesterday.
  • 5. Hai hành động song song: Were Ulla and her friends playing Monopoly while we were playing Draughts?
  • 6. Bối cảnh câu chuyện: The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Lise opened the window and looked out. Phân tích: Past Continuous = bối cảnh, Past Simple = hành động chính
🔥 Công Thức Vàng — Hành Động Bị Gián Đoạn
Past Continuous + when + Past Simple
I was playing on my computer when it suddenly crashed.
Đang chơi (liên tục) thì máy bị hỏng (đột ngột)!
⚠️ LỖI SAI THƯỜNG GẶP
Lỗi 1 — Dùng cho thói quen thường xuyên:
❌When we were on holiday, we were playing volleyball every day.
✅When we were on holiday, we played volleyball every day.
Lỗi 2 — Dùng với stative verbs:
❌I was knowing the answer.
✅I knew the answer.
💡 Mẹo Ghi Nhớ
  • Dấu hiệu: at that time while when all day yesterday
  • Quy tắc: Hành động "đang diễn ra" trong quá khứ → Past Continuous!
2.3

Past Perfect Simple — Thì Quá Khứ Hoàn Thành

🤔 Mở đầu: "Quá khứ của quá khứ" — Nghe có vẻ phức tạp?
Bản chất: Đây là thì "du hành thời gian" — nó đưa chúng ta về trước một thời điểm trong quá khứ. Như xem phim "flashback"!

📝 Công thức:

had + Past Participle (V3)

🎯 Các cách dùng:

  • 1. Trạng thái trước một thời điểm quá khứ: We'd lived next to the gym for a couple of months before I decided to join. Timeline: Sống bên gym (trước) → Quyết định tham gia (sau)
  • 2. Hành động hoàn thành trước thời điểm quá khứ: I'd already bought the computer game when I saw it was cheaper in another shop. Thời gian: Mua game (trước) → Thấy chỗ khác rẻ hơn (sau)
  • 3. Kết quả tại thời điểm quá khứ: We didn't feel like playing Scrabble because we had just finished a long game of Monopoly.
🔥 Điểm Đặc Biệt

It was the second time I'd been on a plane.

Quy tắc: "It was the + số thứ tự + time" + Past Perfect

🎬 Cách Sử Dụng Trong Câu Chuyện

Sau khi dùng Past Perfect một lần, ta thường chuyển sang Past Simple:

I felt pleased when I had had one flying lesson, which was great fun, and I knew immediately that I wanted to get my pilot's licence.

⚠️ LỖI SAI THƯỜNG GẶP
Lỗi 1 — Lạm dụng Past Perfect:
❌After I had finished homework, I had watched TV, then I had gone to bed.
✅After I had finished homework, I watched TV, then I went to bed.
Lỗi 2 — Dùng khi không cần thiết:
❌I had lunch yesterday. (thêm "had had" không cần thiết)
✅I had lunch yesterday.
💡 Mẹo Ghi Nhớ
  • Dấu hiệu: before after when by the time already just
  • Quy tắc: Cần phân biệt "trước-sau" trong quá khứ → Past Perfect!
2.4

Past Perfect Continuous — Thì Quá Khứ Hoàn Thành Tiếp Diễn

🤔 Mở đầu: Nhấn mạnh "quá trình" trong quá khứ?
Bản chất: Kết hợp Past Perfect + Continuous — nhấn mạnh quá trình liên tục trước một thời điểm trong quá khứ.

📝 Công thức:

had been + V-ing

🎯 Các cách dùng:

  • 1. Hành động liên tục đến thời điểm quá khứ: When you saw us, we had been running for six miles – and we still had a mile to go!
  • 2. Hành động vừa dừng trước thời điểm quá khứ: Sârân looked tired because she had been exercising all morning. Dẫn chứng: Mệt mỏi = bằng chứng của việc tập thể dục!
💡 Mẹo Ghi Nhớ
  • Ít dùng hơn các thì khác.
  • Chỉ dùng khi cần nhấn mạnh "quá trình liên tục" trong quá khứ.
2.5

Would — Thói Quen Trong Quá Khứ

🤔 Mở đầu: "Would" không chỉ dùng trong câu điều kiện?
Bản chất: "Would" có thể diễn tả thói quen trong quá khứ, đặc biệt là quá khứ xa xôi, mang tính hoài niệm.

📝 Công thức:

would + V (nguyên mẫu)

🎯 Cách dùng:

  • Thói quen hoài niệm trong quá khứ: When I was very young, my grandfather would take me to the park to play. Cảm giác: Hoài niệm, ấm áp về kỷ niệm xưa
⚠️ LỖI SAI THƯỜNG GẶP
Lỗi 1 — Dùng trong câu hỏi và phủ định:
❌Would you play games together?
✅Did you use to play games together?
Ngoại lệ: "We would never play games together." (được phép)
Lỗi 2 — Dùng với trạng thái:
❌We would have a big house.
✅We used to have a big house.
💡 Mẹo Ghi Nhớ
  • Would: Chỉ dùng cho hành động trong quá khứ, mang tính hoài niệm.
  • Used to: Dùng cho cả hành động và trạng thái.
2.6

Used to — Thói Quen & Trạng Thái Quá Khứ

🤔 Mở đầu: "I used to be shy" vs "I would be shy" — Cái nào đúng?
Bản chất: "Used to" là "chuyên gia" nói về những điều đã từng xảy ra nhưng không còn nữa.

📝 Công thức:

Loại câu Công thức
Khẳng định S + used to + V
Phủ định S + didn't use to + V
Nghi vấn Did + S + use to + V?

🎯 Cách dùng:

  • Thói quen / trạng thái đã từng có: My mother used to play a lot of squash before I was born. Ý nghĩa: Trước kia mẹ chơi squash, bây giờ không còn!

🔄 So sánh Would vs Used To:

🔄 Bảng so sánh
Điểm khác biệt Would Used to
Hành động ✅ ✅
Trạng thái ❌ ✅
Câu hỏi / Phủ định Hạn chế ✅
Cảm xúc Hoài niệm Trung tính

Ví dụ minh hoạ:

  • ✅ Đúng (used to — trạng thái): We used to have a house near the park.
  • ❌ Sai (would — trạng thái): We would have a house near the park.
⚠️ LỖI SAI THƯỜNG GẶP
Lỗi 1 — Nhầm lẫn với "be used to":
❌I used to wake up early.
✅I'm used to waking up early.
(khi muốn nói đã quen với việc dậy sớm)
Lỗi 2 — Viết sai trong câu hỏi:
❌Did you used to play football?
✅Did you use to play football?
2.7

Be / Get Used To — Quen Với Điều Gì Đó

🤔 Mở đầu: "I'm used to it" — Khác gì với "I used to do it"?
Bản chất: Diễn tả việc đã quen hoặc đang quen với một tình huống.

📝 Công thức:

be / get used to + V-ing / noun

🎯 Cách dùng:

  • 1. Be used to — Đã quen (trạng thái): I didn't like being the goalkeeper at first but now I'm used to it.
  • 2. Get used to — Đang quen dần (quá trình): I'm gradually getting used to being in a new team.

🔄 So sánh ba cấu trúc:

🔄 Ba cấu trúc — nhìn một lần, nhớ mãi
Cấu trúc Ý nghĩa Ví dụ
used to + V Đã từng (quá khứ) I used to smoke.
be used to + V-ing Đã quen với I'm used to smoking.
get used to + V-ing Đang quen dần I'm getting used to not smoking.
⚠️ LỖI SAI THƯỜNG GẶP
Lỗi 1 — Nhầm lẫn ba cấu trúc:
❌I used to smoking.
✅I'm used to smoking.
(đã quen)
✅I used to smoke.
(đã từng)
Lỗi 2 — Quên dùng V-ing:
❌I'm used to wake up early.
✅I'm used to waking up early.
💡 Mẹo Ghi Nhớ
  • Used to: Quá khứ (đã từng)
  • Be used to: Hiện tại (đã quen)
  • Get used to: Quá trình (đang quen)

📖 A Memorable Childhood Experience

When I was twelve years old, something extraordinary happened that completely changed my perspective on life. My grandfather used to tell me stories about his adventures, and he would always end each tale with the same advice: "Life rewards the brave." I didn't use to understand what he meant until that summer when I had my own adventure. It was the second time I 'd been to summer camp, but this experience turned out to be nothing like the first.

The morning started like any other at Camp Wildwood. The sun was shining brilliantly, and birds were singing in the tall pine trees surrounding our cabins. I had already packed my backpack for the day's hiking expedition when our counselor, Jake, announced something unexpected. "Today," he said, "we're going to try something different." My friends and I were eating breakfast in the dining hall when he revealed the plan: a wilderness survival challenge. We had been preparing for a regular hike all week, but this was something entirely new. The camp had just introduced this program, and we were going to be the first group to try it.

I remembered how nervous I felt at that moment. Before coming to camp, I had never spent a night outdoors without proper camping equipment. My parents would often take me camping when I was younger, but we always had tents, sleeping bags, and plenty of food. This challenge meant building our own shelters and finding water sources. My teammate Sarah was looking worried too – she had been attending the camp for three years, but she hadn't expected anything like this. "I used to be afraid of the dark," she whispered to me, "and honestly, I still am." I understood her fear because I was feeling the same way.

The instructors divided us into teams of four. My team consisted of Sarah, two boys named Mike and David, and myself. Mike had grown up in the countryside and used to go fishing with his father every weekend. David, on the other hand, had lived in New York City his whole life and had never built anything outdoors before. We were discussing our strategy when Jake approached us. "You guys have been working well together all week," he said encouragingly. "I know you can do this." His words helped, but we were still anxious about what lay ahead.

We set off into the woods around noon. The trail was steep and challenging, and we were carrying only basic supplies: a knife, rope, a water purification kit, and emergency whistles. After we had been hiking for about two hours, we reached our designated area. Other teams had already arrived and were beginning to scout for good shelter locations. Mike immediately took charge – he had watched countless survival videos before coming to camp and knew exactly what to look for. While he and David were searching for the perfect spot, Sarah and I started gathering materials. We were collecting branches and leaves when suddenly it began to rain.

The rain caught us completely off guard. We hadn't checked the weather forecast that morning, and the instructors hadn't mentioned any possibility of rain. Everyone was running around, trying to work faster. I remembered something my grandfather used to say: "When things get tough, that's when you discover who you really are." Those words echoed in my mind as we struggled to build our shelter. We had been working for almost an hour in the rain when Mike had a brilliant idea. He had noticed a fallen tree that created a natural lean-to structure. We quickly modified our plan and used it as the foundation for our shelter.

By evening, we had completed our basic shelter, and the rain had stopped. We were exhausted but proud. David, who had been complaining earlier, was now smiling broadly. "I never thought I could do something like this," he admitted. "Back home, I would spend all my time playing video games. I didn't use to like outdoor activities at all." Sarah was building a fire using the dry wood we had collected and stored under our shelter earlier. She had learned the technique just that morning, but she was doing it perfectly. The fire crackled to life just as darkness was falling.

That night was magical. We were sitting around our small fire, and Mike was teaching us about constellations. He used to study astronomy with his grandmother, who had been a science teacher. The stars seemed brighter than I 'd ever seen them before. We were sharing stories and laughing when we heard something moving in the bushes nearby. My heart was racing, and Sarah grabbed my arm. It turned out to be just a curious raccoon, but for a moment, we had been genuinely scared. After the raccoon had left, we couldn't stop laughing at ourselves.

The next morning, Jake came to check on us. He was impressed with what we had accomplished. "You kids did better than groups twice your age," he told us. I felt incredibly proud. Before this experience, I had never pushed myself so hard or worked so well in a team. My parents didn't use to believe I could handle real challenges – they would always protect me from difficult situations. But that weekend proved that I was capable of much more than anyone had imagined.

When my parents picked me up from camp, they immediately noticed something different about me. "You look more confident," my mother observed. I told them about our adventure, and they were amazed. That experience taught me that I was stronger than I used to think. I had been living in my comfort zone for too long, but that summer changed everything. Now, whenever I face a challenge, I remember that rainy afternoon when four kids built a shelter together and discovered what they were truly capable of. I did win against my fears that day – not just the fear of the wilderness, but the fear of my own limitations.

Grammar Features Highlighted

Past Simple: Used throughout for completed actions (happened, changed, started), story sequences (divided, set off, reached), and past facts (consisted, meant).

Past Continuous: Demonstrated for ongoing actions (was shining, were singing), interrupted actions (were eating when he revealed), parallel actions (were discussing, were collecting), and background descriptions (was falling, was racing).

Past Perfect Simple: Shown for actions before past moments (had already packed, had never spent, had grown up), states before past time (had been a science teacher), and experiences (I'd ever seen).

Past Perfect Continuous: Used for continuous actions before past moments (had been preparing, had been attending, had been working).

Would: Used for past habits with nostalgic feeling (would always end, would often take, would spend, would always protect).

Used to: Correctly used for past habits and states (used to tell, used to study, didn't use to like, used to be afraid).

Be / Get used to: While not heavily featured (as the story is set in the past), the distinction is clear through context.

Special Features: Emphatic "did" (I did win); "It was the second time I'd been" pattern with Past Perfect; story progression using Past Continuous for background and Past Simple for main events.

Common Errors Avoided: Correct irregular verb forms (caught, taught, built); proper auxiliary usage (Did you use to not Did you used to); appropriate tense selection based on context; no Past Continuous with stative verbs.
